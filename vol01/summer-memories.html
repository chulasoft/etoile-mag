Chapter One · Morning
Morning Light
朝の光 — ふたりの朝
Two separate mornings, two different worlds, but somewhere in the same summer light, Akane and Mina were both waking up to the same kind of day.
Mina · Post 01 · 朝の目覚め
"目が覚めたら、ふわっと光が溢れてて。" ☀️
When Mina woke up, the light was already spilling softly across her room, the kind of morning that feels like a gift before the day has asked anything of you.
Soft light everywhere when I opened my eyes. A gentle start.
Morning · Post 01 — Mina04
Akane · Post 01 · 朝の光
"朝の光が、一番きれいなわたしをそっと起こしてくれる。" ♡
Akane stretched into the warmth of the morning light, not yet ready to let the day begin, just letting herself exist in the in-between. Still half in a dream, but smiling already.
Morning light wakes the prettiest version of me, gently.

Mina · Post 02
"今日の気分は、さっぱり塩レモンパスタ！" 🍋
Mina cooked before most people were even awake, the smell of lemon filling the kitchen before the day had properly started.
Feeling like lemon pasta today. Hope it turns out right. ♪
Morning · Post 02 — Mina06
Akane · Post 02
"お気に入りの音楽と、淹れたてのコーヒーで一日が始まる。" ☕
Akane poured slowly, deliberately, the way she did everything she actually cared about. Favourite music, fresh coffee, morning light on her shoulders.
Favourite music, fresh coffee. That's how today begins.

Chapter Two · Together
Poolside Days
青い水面 — ふたりの時間
By afternoon their separate mornings had led them to the same place. The water was cold and impossibly blue. Neither of them remembered whose idea it was to come here.

Coffee, sweets, and sunshine. The perfect afternoon, together.

Just here. Just this. It feels so good. ♡

Akane · Post 04

Akane at the pool's edge with a pink cocktail in hand, the shimmer of the water behind her, everything warm and unhurried.
Every shimmer on the water lifts something in me. Summer magic. ✦

Chapter Three · Golden Hour
Golden Hour
夕暮れの光 — ふたりの海
By evening the light had turned gold. They found themselves at the shore, watching the last of summer burn itself out across the water.
Akane · Post 03
"波の音、風の匂い、太陽のぬくもり。" ♡
Akane walked the shore with her hat in hand, the sea breeze catching her hair, free in a way that only the beach can make you feel.
The sound of waves, the scent of wind, the warmth of sun. All of it sets me free.
Beach · Post 03 — Akane20
Mina · Post 03
"海風が、ふわりと髪を揺らしていく。" ♡
The sea wind moved through Mina's hair and she felt, suddenly, very light, like the summer had been slowly teaching her something she couldn't quite name yet.
The sea wind drifts softly through my hair. This moment is mine. ✦

Together · Last Scene
"この夏を、忘れない。" ✦
Walking together as the sun went down, the sand still warm underfoot, the sky turning every colour at once. Neither of them spoke. They didn't need to.
We won't forget this summer. The shoreline we walked, side by side.

Mina's summer has a different texture, fresh, light, and full of small joys. She finds magic in ordinary mornings and turns every afternoon into something worth remembering.

She's already been awake for an hour. She'll never admit that. She already knows what she wants for breakfast, what music she'll play, and exactly how to make you smile before noon.
おはよー。よく眠れた？
