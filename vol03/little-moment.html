Chapter One · Morning
Slow Mornings
朝の章 · The Beginning
Some mornings are too soft to rush. Nuna knows this. She watches Rina move through the light like the day belongs to her, and honestly, it does.
She starts every morning the same way. A stretch, a yawn, and Nuna already sitting there waiting. As if the whole day was planned around her schedule.
Rina · Post 01 · Morning Routine
"静かな朝は、少しだけ時間がゆっくり流れる。" ☀️
"She never rushes. I respect that about her." ♡
Some mornings are too soft to rush. The light comes in warm, Nuna is still asleep, and the day hasn't asked anything of anyone yet.
Nuna woke up before the alarm. She always does. She watched Rina breathe slowly in the warm morning light and decided, generously, to let her sleep five more minutes.
Some mornings are too soft to rush.
静かな朝は、少しだけ時間がゆっくり流れる。
Morning · Post 01 — Rina01
Rina · Post 02 · Before The Shower
"静かな朝は、いつも誰かがそばにいる。" ♡
"She never showers alone. Nuna makes sure of that." ☁️
You never really shower alone with a cat nearby. Nuna followed her to the bathroom door, sat down, and made it very clear that supervision was required.
Nuna followed her to the bathroom. Not because she wanted anything. Just because someone had to keep an eye on things. Professionally.
You never really shower alone with a cat nearby.
静かな朝は、いつも誰かがそばにいる。
Morning · Post 02 — Rina02
Rina · Post 03 · Getting Ready
"静かな朝の支度が、今日をやさしく整えていく。" ✦
"She stood at the mirror like she had all the time in the world." ✦
Quiet mornings begin with small familiar rituals. The warmth of a towel. The soft light through the curtain. Nuna sitting beside the mirror, watching everything.
Rina always gets ready slowly. Each step deliberate, unhurried. Nuna sat close and watched without judgment. This is what trust looks like between them.
Quiet mornings begin with small familiar rituals.
静かな朝の支度が、今日をやさしく整えていく。
Morning · Post 03 — Rina03
Rina · Post 03b · Dressing Room
"今日のわたしを、ていねいに選んでいく。" ♡
She picks her clothes the way she picks her mornings, carefully, without hurry. Nuna sat on the dresser and watched with an expression that said she had opinions but would keep them to herself.
Choosing today's version of herself, one piece at a time.

Chapter II · キッチンの時間
II
Chapter Two · Kitchen
Cooking Together
キッチンの章 · Small Interruptions
Every quiet morning becomes a little brighter with small interruptions. Nuna doesn't help. But she makes everything more interesting.
The kitchen is Nuna's second favorite room. Something is always happening there. And where something happens, Nuna belongs.
Rina · Post 04 · Cooking Together
"静かな朝も、小さないたずらで少しだけ賑やかになる。" 🍳
"The kitchen is more interesting when Nuna decides to help." 🐾
Every quiet morning becomes brighter with small interruptions. Nuna jumped onto the counter, extended one paw, and looked Rina directly in the eye. An offering. Or a demand.
Nuna joined her at the stove. Not to cause trouble. To assist. The distinction is important. Rina didn't seem to understand the distinction, but that's fine.
Every quiet morning becomes brighter with small interruptions.
静かな朝も、小さないたずらで少しだけ賑やかになる。
Kitchen · Post 04 — Rina05
Rina · Post 05 · Breakfast For Two
"何気ない朝ほど、やさしい時間が流れている。" ♡
"She always feeds Nuna first. Every single morning." 🐾
The quietest mornings are often the ones most worth keeping. Rina knelt on the floor with Nuna's bowl and stayed there a little longer than necessary.
Rina kneeled on the floor every morning to serve breakfast. Nuna considered this very appropriate. She ate with dignity, as always.
The quietest mornings are often shared.
何気ない朝ほど、やさしい時間が流れている。
Kitchen · Post 05 — Rina06
Rina · Post 06 · Can I Have Some?
"静かな朝ごはんも、少しだけ賑やかなほうが好き。" ✌️
"Nuna already had breakfast. This was just bonus research." 🍞
Breakfast always tastes better when someone wants to steal it. Nuna reached for the toast with one careful paw. Rina laughed. Nuna took this as a yes.
Nuna raised one paw with the precision of a surgeon. Rina had the toast. Nuna simply needed to verify it was safe to eat. For science.
Breakfast always tastes better with a little thief nearby.
静かな朝ごはんも、少しだけ賑やかなほうが好き。

Chapter Three · Workout
Workout Interrupted
ワークアウトの章 · Manager on Duty
Every peaceful routine eventually belongs to the cat. Rina accepted this a long time ago. Nuna never asked permission.
The yoga mat is a shared resource. Rina uses it for exercise. Nuna uses it for supervision. There is no conflict here.
Rina · Post 07 · Workout Interruption
"静かな時間ほど、なぜか邪魔したくなるらしい。" 😄
"Nuna found the perfect spot. Rina's back, during planks." 🐾
Every peaceful routine eventually belongs to the cat. Nuna settled onto her back mid-plank, closed her eyes, and began to purr. Rina held the position. She didn't want to wake her.
Rina was mid-plank when Nuna climbed on. Perfectly flat surface, warm, gently breathing. Nuna approved. She stayed for three full sets. Rina didn't complain once.
Every peaceful routine eventually belongs to the cat.
静かな時間ほど、なぜか邪魔したくなるらしい。
Workout · Post 07 — Rina08
Rina · Post 08 · Please Move, Manager
"作業を始めるたびに、なぜか一番いい場所を取られてしまう。" ☕
"The keyboard is Nuna's desk now. Rina uses the edges." 💻
Every time she sat down to work, the keyboard was already taken. Nuna settled in with quiet authority. Rina reached around her and typed with considerable patience.
Nuna claimed the keyboard at 9:47am. Rina put on her headphones, leaned over, and worked around the situation. This is called adaptation. Nuna calls it progress.
The keyboard belongs to the cat now. She just borrows it.
作業を始めるたびに、なぜか一番いい場所を取られてしまう。

IV
Chapter Four · Poolside
Poolside Hours
プールの章 · Supervised Relaxation
Even quiet afternoons need proper supervision. Nuna arrived at the poolside and took her position. The afternoon could begin.
Rina brought Nuna to the pool. Or Nuna decided to come. The distinction, again, is important.
Rina · Post 09 · Poolside
"静かな午後も、ちゃんと見守られているらしい。" ☀️
"They matched. Orange hair, orange fur. Neither planned it." 🕶️
Even quiet afternoons need proper supervision. Nuna watched from the edge as Rina waded into the water. She did not get in. She supervised.
Rina put on her sunglasses. Nuna was already wearing hers. Some afternoons feel brighter with matching company. Neither of them said anything about it.
Even quiet afternoons need proper supervision.
静かな午後も、ちゃんと見守られているらしい。
Poolside · Post 09 — Rina10
Chapter V · 夜のおわりに
V
Chapter Five · Evening
Warm Evenings
夜の章 · Goodnight, Supervisor
The quietest evenings are always the warmest. There is nothing that needs doing. Nothing that needs saying. Just the lamp on, the city outside, and the two of them.
Rina · Post 10 · Warm Evening Steam
"湯気に包まれる夜は、心まで少しやわらかくなる。" ✦
"She always looks softer in the evenings. Steam and candlelight." ✦
The quietest evenings are always the warmest. She sank into the water as the city lights blurred beyond the steam. Nuna sat close, sleepy, content.
Nuna sat on the towel at the edge of the onsen and watched Rina disappear into the steam. She stayed until Rina came back. This is what Nuna calls loyalty.
On nights wrapped in steam, something in the heart softens.
湯気に包まれる夜は、心まで少しやわらかくなる。
Evening · Post 10 — Rina11
Rina · Post 11 · Five More Minutes
"眠る前の少しだけが、いつも一番長く感じる。" ♡
"Rina always says five more minutes. Nuna never argues." ♡
She was supposed to sleep five minutes ago. Instead, Nuna rolled onto her back and looked up, and Rina stayed a little longer. The night can wait.
Rina pulled Nuna closer and held on. Nuna let her. This is Nuna's greatest gift: knowing exactly when to stay still.
The minutes before sleep always feel the longest.
眠る前の少しだけが、いつも一番長く感じる。
Evening · Post 11 — Rina12
Rina · Post 12 · Goodnight, Supervisor
"気づけば、今日も一緒に眠ってしまっていた。" ✦
"We both lost the battle against sleep. Together, as usual." ✦
We both lost the battle against sleep. The city was still outside, the lamp was still on, and neither of them noticed. They were already somewhere else.
Rina fell asleep first tonight. Nuna waited, then settled in beside her. Another day done. The supervisor approved of this outcome.
Without noticing, we fell asleep together again.
気づけば、今日も一緒に眠ってしまっていた。
